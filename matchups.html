Know the assumptions

A forecast is a distribution.

The model description and coverage will appear when the saved data loads.

Fair lines

Moneylines split simulated tie probability evenly and convert that share into American odds, with no added margin. Spread, total, and prop prices exclude pushes. Win, tie, and push probabilities are disclosed separately.

Current roster assumption

Each week selects the legal lineup with the highest expected points from the full current roster, including the bench. Reserve and taxi players are excluded. Known NFL byes are applied, and future weeks hold the roster rather than submitted starters. Roster changes and news can change results. Unknown playoff pairings are not invented.

Reading player props

Over/under refers to fantasy points under this league’s scoring. A 15+ threshold means at least 15 fantasy points. Sparse observations and positional priors are marked.
